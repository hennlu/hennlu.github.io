People and research

Students & postdocs

Information for prospective master's students, PhD applicants and postdoctoral researchers interested in geometric representation theory.

Research environment

I am a CNRS researcher at LAMFA, Université de Picardie Jules Verne, in Amiens. My research covers cohomological Hall algebras, BPS Lie algebras, moduli spaces, quiver representations and related questions in algebraic geometry.

The ANR JCJC project ReSCoS (2027–2031) includes planned support for a doctoral and a postdoctoral researcher.

Open positions

No specific call is announced on this page at present. Details of future posts, eligibility requirements and application deadlines will be published here once confirmed. This page should not be interpreted as a currently open recruitment announcement.

Potential topics

Topics may include moduli stacks and symplectic geometry, BPS cohomology and cohomological Hall algebras, nonabelian Hodge theory, or quiver representation theory. The precise project will depend on the student's background and interests.

Contact

If you would like to discuss a potential research project, please contact me with a brief description of your mathematical background and interests: Lucien.Hennecart@u-picardie.fr.
