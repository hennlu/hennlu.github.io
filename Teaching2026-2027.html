Advanced course · 2026–2027

Perverse sheaves

M2 Mathématiques Fondamentales · 1 March – 9 April 2027

Course information

This advanced course introduces perverse sheaves, their construction and key operations, and their role in modern algebraic geometry and geometric representation theory.

Dates: 1 March – 9 April 2027.

Audience: Master's and PhD students. Programme: M2 Mathématiques Fondamentales, Sorbonne Université, Université Paris Cité and Université Sorbonne Paris Nord.
