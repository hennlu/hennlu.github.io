Project

Presentation

ReSCoS is a research project funded by the French National Research Agency through the JCJC programme.

The project concerns geometric representation theory and the study of algebraic and geometric structures arising from moduli spaces, cohomological Hall algebras, BPS invariants and Calabi-Yau categories.



Scientific objectives

The main objectives of the project are the following.

  • Study cohomological Hall algebras and BPS Lie algebras.
  • Investigate moduli spaces arising in geometric representation theory.
  • Develop interactions with Donaldson-Thomas theory.
  • Support collaborations, research visits and scientific events.
