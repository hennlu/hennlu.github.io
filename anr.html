ANR JCJC · 2027–2031

ReSCoS

A five-year project in geometric representation theory, led by Lucien Hennecart at CNRS and LAMFA in Amiens.

About the project

ReSCoS is supported by the French National Research Agency (ANR) through the JCJC programme. The project runs from 1 January 2027 to 31 December 2031.

Its scientific programme concerns the interaction between the geometry and cohomology of moduli stacks and representation-theoretic structures, particularly cohomological Hall algebras (CoHAs), BPS cohomology, Calabi–Yau categories and related counting problems.

The project supports collaborative research, visits and research activities as well as a planned PhD and postdoctoral position.

Scientific programme

Research directions

Geometry of moduli stacks

Developing geometric techniques for studying the cohomology of moduli stacks, with particular attention to symplectic structures and integrality phenomena.

CoHAs and BPS structures

Understanding the structure of cohomological Hall algebras and BPS invariants, as well as the Lie-theoretic constructions they encode.

Counting and representation theory

Connecting geometric and cohomological invariants to enumerative questions for representations, quivers and related moduli problems.

Interactions and applications

Exploring links between these methods and Calabi–Yau geometry, Donaldson–Thomas theory and other geometric representation-theoretic constructions.
